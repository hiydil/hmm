# sonderland

*Noun (Coinage)*. The imaginary geographic landscape that forms when you look out of a train window or balcony at night and realize that every glowing apartment window houses a consciousness as intricate, panicked, and vivid as your own.

---

### Origin & Texture
Formed by crossing *sonder* (the realization that each passerby has a life as vivid as your own) with *land*. It represents not just an ephemeral emotion, but a physical territory of simultaneous solitude.
Related in spirit to the invented German term @de.coinage(Halbmund) and Turkish @tr.coinage(özdüşüm).
