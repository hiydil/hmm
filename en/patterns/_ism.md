# -ism

A suffix that turns a fluid behavior, feeling, or observation into an ideological system or frozen doctrine.

When you append `-ism` to a root word, you erect scaffolding around an idea. It signals that someone has gathered principles, codified them into rules, and likely started arguing with others who hold a competing `-ism`.

---

### Observed Dynamics
Watch how an innocent impulse transforms:
- `hero` &rarr; `heroism` (the living act becomes a virtue standard)
- `ideal` &rarr; `idealism` (a personal longing turns into an epistemological camp)
