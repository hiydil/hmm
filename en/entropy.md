# entropy

A measure of dispersed possibilities. Imagine a clean desk: there is only one configuration where every pen, book, and mug is in its exact assigned coordinates. But there are quintillions of ways for things to be scattered in disarray. Entropy is simply the overwhelming statistical probability of disorder.

Left to itself, heat disperses, structures erode, and rooms get messy—not because nature has malice, but because there are vastly more ways for energy to be spread out than concentrated.

### Psychological Entropy
The natural unraveling of mental clarity when no energy is put into maintaining inner stillness. Without deliberate reflection, thoughts drift into worry and fragmentation. In Turkish, maintaining alignment with one's inner feeling is captured by the elusive state of @tr(gönül).
