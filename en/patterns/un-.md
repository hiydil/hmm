# un-

The subtractive prefix of reversal and absence. It doesn't just negate a quality; it often implies an undoing—a rewind button pressed on a state of being.

---

### Inversion Mechanism
Notice the difference between `non-` (neutral classification) and `un-` (active tension or disruption):
- *Non-done* is a status.
- *Undone* carries emotional weight, dissolution, unraveling.
Similar to the disruptive or errant German prefix @de.pattern(ver-).
